Rage Quit Penalty Rule

Official rules regarding unjustified match abandonment.

📢 OFFICIAL NEW RULE — RAGE QUIT PENALTY

In order to maintain competitiveness, respect between players, and the integrity of the monthly ranking, the following rules will now apply to unjustified quits in 4vs matches.

⚠️ UNJUSTIFIED QUIT = -40 RATING

Any player who leaves a round without a valid reason will automatically receive a penalty of -40 Rating points.

For the penalty to be applied:

  • ✅ The 3 remaining players must agree that it was effectively a rage quit / unjustified abandonment.
  • ✅ This must be indicated through a note alongside the posted score.

🚫 RANKING SUSPENSION

If a player accumulates 3 unjustified quits during the same ranking month:

⛔ The player will be suspended from the ranking for 3 weeks.

This means:

  • The player may continue playing.
  • However, any rounds they participate in during the suspension period will NOT count toward the monthly ranking.

🏁 ROUND VALIDATION OR CANCELLATION

📍 If the quit happens after Track 8 or later:

  • The 3 remaining players may agree to:
    • continue playing 3vs,
    • recalculate the final score,
    • or completely void the round.

📍 If the quit happens before Track 8:

  • The round is automatically canceled.
  • The quitting player will still receive the -40 Rating penalty.

In both situations, the offending player will not receive any additional points in the final score.

🎯 PURPOSE OF THIS RULE

These measures are intended to:

  • protect ladder competitiveness,
  • prevent result manipulation,
  • encourage commitment once a round begins,
  • and maintain a serious and fair environment for all players.

Thank you for supporting the competitive growth of the community. 🏆
